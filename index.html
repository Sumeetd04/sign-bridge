Learn: Do's & Don'ts

✅ Do's of Sign Language Etiquette

  • Maintain eye contact when a deaf person is signing to you.
  • Learn basic signs like "hello," "thank you," and "please."
  • Be patient and understanding if communication is challenging.
  • Use gestures, writing, or pointing if a sign is unknown.
  • Attract attention politely by waving or tapping on the shoulder.
  • Ask "How may I help you?" rather than assuming assistance is needed.

❌ Don'ts of Sign Language Etiquette

  • Don't interrupt a signed conversation by walking between signers.
  • Don't assume all deaf people can read lips or prefer to speak.
  • Don't be afraid to make mistakes; it's part of learning.
  • Don't shout or exaggerate your speech.
  • Don't touch a deaf person without their permission.
  • Don't block your mouth or face when speaking to a lip-reader.
